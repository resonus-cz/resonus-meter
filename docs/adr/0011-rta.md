# ADR 0011: RTA design and preferences

## Context

Milestone M5 adds the octave and one-third-octave analyser of
specification 4.6 with its tab (5.3) and preferences (5.4). Each new setting
needs a short ADR note (specification 7, rule 5).

## Decision

- `spl-dsp::filterbank` implements the bands with 6th-order Butterworth
  band-passes and octave-wise decimation. Bands run at the lowest rate where
  their upper edge is at most 1/8 (one-third octave) or 1/16 (octave) of it,
  and the top bands on a 2×-oversampled input, as often as needed. The first
  design (1/4 of the rate, no oversampling) read the 16 kHz octave band 13 dB
  away from the analog response; see `docs/DSP_NOTES.md`.
- The engine analyses the unweighted input while the RTA tab is visible
  (specification 3.3), and applies A or C display weighting as a constant per
  band at the exact mid-band frequency (IEC 61672-1 Annex E response).
- Band Leq starts when the tab is shown, and restarts on Reset and New
  Session; it holds during a pause, like the meter.
- Peak hold lives in the RTA tab, not the engine: it is a display aid. It is
  cleared by Reset, New Session and any change of detector, weighting or bands.
- Preferences, under `[rta]` in `settings.toml` with defaults (schema
  version stays 1): bandwidth, lowest and highest band (nominal one-third-
  octave frequencies), and the tab's default detector, weighting and peak
  hold. Defaults are applied to the tab when changed.

## Consequences

The analyser costs nothing while hidden. Band Leq covers only the time the
tab was shown, which the tab states. The RTA is not saved with measurements
yet; the requested RTA log is a planned item.
